# Edu_career_success

🎓📈 **Education & Career Success Analysis**

This project explores the relationship between education, personal factors, and early career success using a dataset of 5,000 students. Through data analysis, statistical insights, and visualizations, we uncover patterns around salary, job satisfaction, field of study, and more.

📁 **Dataset**

The dataset (education_career_success.csv) includes student-level information:

Student_ID, Age, Gender

Field_of_Study, University_GPA, SAT_Score

Projects_Completed, Entrepreneurship interest

Starting_Salary, Career_Satisfaction, Current_Job_Level

🛠️ **Technologies Used**

Python (Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, Matplotlib)

Jupyter Notebook

Scikit-learn (for encoding categorical data)

🔍 **Key Analyses**

📊 **Statistical Insights:**

Average salary varies by field of study, peaking in Field 0 (~$51,423).

Only 20% of students show interest in entrepreneurship.

Projects completed and starting salary have weak correlation (+0.015).

Career satisfaction is highest in Field 5 and lowest in Field 1.

Minor gender and age-based differences in income were observed.

📈 **Visualizations:**

Histograms show a normal distribution of starting salaries.

Boxplots illustrate salary comparisons across gender.

Scatterplots examine the relationship between GPA and SAT scores.

Bar charts reveal satisfaction trends by field.

Pie charts show near-even gender distribution.

📌 **Conclusion**

This project offers data-driven insights into how education paths and experiences influence early career outcomes. It highlights the importance of field choice, personal projects, and job level entry points in shaping success.
